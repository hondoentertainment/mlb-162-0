# Top teams in 162-0

One Franchise lets you lock a club for all nine rounds. Each spin then draws a decade from that club’s history. This guide ranks the **strongest locks** in the current pool and the **franchise-decades** you most want to see in Classic, Daily, or Era Lock.

Counts below are unique people in the catalog (a name who appears in two Yankee decades still counts once for the franchise). Hall of Fame names are actual HOFers. Every playable club era can field a starting nine (C through SP).

**Play:** [162-amber.vercel.app](https://162-amber.vercel.app)

---

## How choosing a team works

- **One Franchise** — you pick the club. The game only spins decades that franchise actually has (eight for the original clubs, four to seven for expansion).
- **Era Lock** — you pick the decade. The game spins franchises that existed then.
- **Classic / Daily / Challenge / Ironman** — both reels are random. You do not pick a team up front; you draft whoever that spin lists.

A deep franchise is not a free 162-0. Nine random decades can still miss the golden era, and a player can only be drafted once even if he shows up again later.

## Best One Franchise locks

These clubs have the most Hall of Famers, the most tier-4/5 stars, and useful names in almost every decade.

### 1. New York Yankees

The deepest book in the game: **14 Hall of Famers**, ten tier-5 names, eight eras.

Mantle, Berra, and Ford headline the 1950s. Jeter and Rivera headline the 1990s–2000s. Jackson, Winfield, Henderson, and Mattingly cover the middle decades. You can still spin a quieter 2010s or 2020s card — skip those if you are hunting a Dynasty.

### 2. Atlanta Braves

**13 Hall of Famers** from Milwaukee through Atlanta.

Aaron, Mathews, and Spahn in the 1950s. The 1990s rotation of Maddux, Glavine, and Smoltz with Chipper and McGriff is one of the best single spins in the pool. 2020s Acuña / Olson / Riley / Strider is a modern star card without the HOF badges.

### 3. Los Angeles Dodgers

**11 Hall of Famers**, and the 1950s Brooklyn card is the richest single era in the catalog (Robinson, Campanella, Snider, Reese, Hodges, Koufax’s decade next door).

2020s Betts / Freeman / Ohtani is the other monster Dodger spin. Koufax and Drysdale sit in the 1960s; Piazza and the 1980s staff fill the middle.

### 4. Boston Red Sox

Fewer HOFers than the three clubs above (**8**), but the **highest-scoring all-time nine** in the pool: Williams, Yaz, Fisk, Boggs, Pedro, Ortiz, Rice.

The 1970s (Fisk, Rice, Lynn, Evans, Tiant) and 2000s (Ortiz, Manny, Pedroia, Schilling) are the spins you want. Two decade skips matter here — burn them on a thin 2020s or 1960s card if the stars are already gone.

### 5. Baltimore Orioles

**9 Hall of Famers** and a clean through-line: Brooks and Frank Robinson, Palmer, Murray, Ripken, Alomar, Mussina.

The 1960s–70s and 1990s are the loaded cards. 2020s Henderson / Rutschman / Burnes is a strong modern nine if you have not filled those slots.

### 6. St. Louis Cardinals

Musial, Gibson, Brock, Ozzie, Simmons, Rolen, plus Pujols / Molina / Edmonds in the 2000s.

Eight eras, no real dead decade. The 1960s (Gibson, Brock, Flood) and 2000s are the peaks.

### 7. San Francisco Giants

Mays, McCovey, Marichal, Cepeda, and Perry on the 1960s card. Bonds, Kent, and the 2010s (Posey, Bumgarner, Lincecum, Cain) keep later decades playable.

### 8. Cincinnati Reds

The Big Red Machine is the reason to lock Cincinnati: Bench, Morgan, Perez, Rose, Foster, Concepcion, Gullett on one 1970s spin. Larkin, Griffey Jr., and Robinson cover the other eras.

## Specialist locks

Fewer eras, but one or two spins can carry a season.

- **Mariners** (6 eras) — 1990s Griffey, A-Rod, Randy Johnson, Edgar, Buhner. The single best expansion-era card.
- **A’s** — 1970s dynasty (Reggie, Hunter, Fingers, Campaneris, Rudi) and the 1980s (Henderson, Canseco, McGwire, Eckersley). 1950s is Philadelphia / Kansas City history.
- **Mets** — Seaver / Koosman, the 1980s (Gooden, Strawberry, Carter, Hernandez), Piazza / Wright / Beltran.
- **Angels** — Ryan, Carew, Vlad, Trout, Ohtani. Seven eras from the 1960s.
- **Rangers** — Ryan, Pudge, A-Rod, Beltre. 1960s is the second Washington Senators.

## Notes

- **Washington** in this game is the Nationals plus Expos history (from 1969). The 1960s Texas card is the second Senators.
- **Athletics** 1950s is Philadelphia / Kansas City. **Royals** begin in 1969.
- Approximate batting and pitching lines are marked “approx” in the draft. Tiers and HOF badges are what the sim actually weights.
- The pool is generated from `scripts/` catalogs. Re-run `node scripts/generate-players.mjs` after catalog edits; do not hand-edit `src/data/players.ts`.
